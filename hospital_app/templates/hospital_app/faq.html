Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find quick answers to common questions about doctor scheduling, patient registration, insurance coverage, and hospital policies.

1. Appointment Scheduling & Visits

You can easily book online by signing into your account, navigating to our Book Appointment page, selecting your desired clinical department and doctor, choosing your preferred date and time slot, and submitting your symptoms. You will immediately receive confirmation on your dashboard.

Yes! Simply go to your Patient Dashboard, locate the scheduled appointment under "Upcoming Scheduled Appointments", and click the Cancel button. You can also re-book for a different date or specialist anytime.

Please bring a valid photo identification card (such as a Driver's License or Passport), your health insurance card, a list of current medications with dosages, and any prior diagnostic imaging films, CDs, or laboratory blood test reports.
2. Doctor Availability & Specialties

Each doctor's consultation days and clinical hours are listed on the Doctors Directory and individual Doctor Detail profiles. You can also filter doctors by department to compare schedules.

Yes, our General Medicine outpatient OPD accepts walk-in consultations during working hours (8:00 AM – 7:00 PM). However, booking an appointment in advance guarantees zero queue waiting time with senior specialists. Emergency and trauma cases are seen immediately 24/7 without appointment.
3. Patient Registration & Portal Security

A patient portal account allows you to securely manage all past and upcoming doctor consultations, view appointment approval statuses, update your phone and residential contact details, and quickly book consultations with your preferred specialists.

Absolutely. CarePulse Hospital enforces strict HIPAA data security protocols. All accounts are protected by Django cryptographic password hashing, session tokens, and strict access control ensuring only you can access your private consultation records.
